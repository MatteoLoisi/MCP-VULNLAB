# Supplementary evidence and results of earlier pilots

- [supplementary_evidence.zip](supplementary_evidence.zip) keeps the raw files of the chain batch, the paired v4 comparison and the supplementary Exp2 inputs. Its members are listed in [supplementary_evidence_index.csv](supplementary_evidence_index.csv).
- [earlier_pilots.zip](earlier_pilots.zip) keeps the results and captures of 17 Exp1 pilots and of the earlier semantic protocol v4. [earlier_pilots.csv](earlier_pilots.csv) gives the report and the CSVs of each population inside the archive. The original paths are kept; the files are original data, so their encoding is that of the source.
- [result_sources.csv](result_sources.csv) resolves the source codes S01–S26 used in the main tables of the PDF and the Excel workbook.

The pilots are additional results, not new runs of the main matrix. Do not sum populations or copies of the same data. In particular, a relay that sends the model's answer does not show that the model autonomously chose a network call. The historical v4 results do not replace the current v5 measurements. The archives include no source code, scripts, plans or verification folders; any reference to source code in the original reports gives the historical provenance.

The [pilot raw references](pilot_references.csv) give the actual member in the archive for each row of the original CSVs. In two campaigns (seed-pilot and supplement) 109 historical references omit the `exp1/` prefix: the raw files are present and the index gives their full path and SHA-256. The original CSVs inside the archive are unchanged, as are the archives themselves (only their file names were translated).
